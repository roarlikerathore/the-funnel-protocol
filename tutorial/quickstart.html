Quickstart

Build your funnel in 7 steps

Every click, in order. No coding, no jargon. Follow it straight through and stop when you reach the end.

~20 minutes 2 free accounts No card needed
1

Get your own copy

2 min

On GitHub

  1. Open github.com/roarlikerathore/the-funnel-protocol Type it into your browser, or click the link you were sent.
  2. Sign inNo account? Click Sign up. It is free and takes a minute.
  3. Click the green “Use this template” buttonTop right of the page.
  4. Click “Create a new repository”
  5. Type a nameAnything. For example my-funnel.
  6. Select “Private”
  7. Click “Create repository”
No green button? You are not signed in. Sign in, reload the page, and it appears.
2

Open it in Lovable

3 min

In Lovable

  1. Go to lovable.dev and sign inFree account, no card.
  2. Click “New Project”
  3. Choose “Import from GitHub”
  4. Click “Authorise”This lets Lovable see the copy you just made.
  5. Pick the repository you created in step 1
  6. Say yes to Lovable CloudThat is your database. The funnel does not work without it.
It looks broken and says PLACEHOLDER everywhere. That is correct. You have the machine. Your words go in next.

4

Make the emails personal

5 min

In Lovable

  1. Copy the text below
  2. Paste it into Lovable’s chat and send
  3. Wait until it says it is done
Paste into Lovable
Open supabase/seed-blocks.sql and fill in the block bodies. Write them in my
voice, using the exact profile and challenge wording already in
src/funnel.config.ts. Do not change any other file.

For EACH of my 6 profiles, write PROFILE_MIRROR: two or three short lines
describing that person's actual day back to them. No advice, no pitch.

For EACH of my 6 profiles, write CTA_LINE: one line on why turning up matters
for that specific person.

For EACH of my 5 challenges, write PAIN_TWIST: two or three lines naming the
struggle, then reframing it as a structural problem, not a personal failing.

For EACH of my 5 challenges, write PROOF: two or three lines of reasoning for
why that struggle happens. No statistics, no case studies, no client results.

Rules:
- Plain sentences. Short lines. No exclamation marks, no hype words.
- Never invent a number, a client, or a result.
- Do not give away any session's reveal.
- Replace every NEEDS-COPY. Tell me if any are left.
This is what makes the emails work. Everyone picked a situation and a struggle when they registered. This lets each email answer the one they chose.
5

Build the database

3 min

In Lovable

  1. Copy the text below
  2. Paste it into Lovable’s chat and send
  3. Check it lists four jobs at the end
Paste into Lovable
Read src/funnel.config.ts, then:

1. Run scripts/build-emails.mjs to generate the email SQL.
2. Combine, in this order, into one file called SETUP.sql at the project root:
   sql/01-schema.sql, then sql/02-seeds.sql.tpl with every {{TOKEN}} replaced
   from funnel.config.ts, then the generated email SQL, then
   supabase/seed-blocks.sql, then sql/03-cron.sql.tpl with my project URL and
   anon key filled in.
3. Confirm no {{TOKEN}} remains anywhere in SETUP.sql. Tell me if any does.
4. Run SETUP.sql against my database.
5. Then run this and show me the result:
   SELECT jobname, schedule, active FROM cron.job;

Do not change any other file.
You should see four jobs. They are what actually send your emails. Without them everything piles up and nothing ever goes out.
