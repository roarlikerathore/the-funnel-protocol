Quickstart · Part Two

AI reminder calls

Your funnel is live and taking registrations. This is the single biggest thing you can add next: a short automated call before you go live.

~15 minutes 1 paid account Do it once
—

Why calls, before anything else

People register for a free event and then their week happens. An email is easy to ignore. A phone call thirty minutes before you go live is not.

It is the only remaining thing that changes how many people are actually in the room, rather than how many signed up. Everything else on this page is smaller.

This costs money. Calls are billed per minute by the calling provider. Budget for it before you switch it on, and start with one event rather than a standing setting you forget about.
1

Make a Bolna account

5 min

On bolna.ai

  1. Go to bolna.ai and sign up
  2. Add creditStart small. You are testing, not launching.
  3. Create an agentThis is the voice that will call your registrants.
  4. Copy the Agent IDA long code. You need it in step 3.
  5. Go to Settings → API Keys and create a keyCopy it now. It is shown once.
2

Write the call script

5 min

On bolna.ai

Paste this into your agent, replacing the brackets. Keep it plain.

Your agent script
You are calling someone who signed up for a free online session.

Say this, naturally, and keep it under twenty seconds:

"Hi, is that [first name]? This is a quick reminder from [YOUR NAME]'s team.
Your free online session starts in about thirty minutes. You'll find the
joining link in your email. Will you be able to join us?"

If they say yes: "Great, see you there." End the call.
If they say no: "No problem, thanks for letting me know." End the call.
If they ask a question you cannot answer: "I'll have someone email you
about that." End the call.

Never make promises about results, money, or outcomes.
Never pressure anyone. If they want to go, let them go.
Voice providers check your script, and they are strict. Scripts get rejected for words that are harmless in context. Avoid anything that sounds like a money guarantee, anything about earnings, and unverifiable claims like “the top 3%”. If your brand name itself sounds dramatic, use your event name instead. Plain and boring gets approved. Clever does not.
3

Connect it to your funnel

3 min

In your Control Room

  1. Open your site and add /control-room to the address For example yourdomain.com/control-room
  2. Click “Email me a code”, then enter the code
  3. Go to the Settings tab
  4. Fill in these five values bolna_enabled → true
    bolna_agent_id → your Agent ID from step 1
    bolna_api_key → your API key from step 1
    bolna_lead_time_minutes → 30
    bolna_default_country_code → your country code, e.g. +91
  5. Click away from each field to save it
Your key is safe. It is stored where only the backend can read it, never in a file and never anywhere a visitor's browser can reach.
4

Test it on yourself

2 min

On your own site

  1. Register on your own funnel, with your real phone number
  2. Wait for the call It comes thirty minutes before your event starts. If your event is further away than that, temporarily set the event date to today to test.
  3. Answer it and listen to the whole thing You are checking it sounds like a person, not a robot reading a form.
  4. Check the Control Room shows the call
Do not skip this. The first time you hear your own script out loud is when you notice what is wrong with it. Never let a registrant be the first person to hear it.
5

Two smaller things worth doing

10 min

In your Control Room

Auto-register people on Zoom. Each person gets their own joining link, which means Zoom can tell you who actually turned up.

  1. In Zoom, create a Server-to-Server OAuth app marketplace.zoom.us → Develop → Build App
  2. Give it these three permissions webinar:read, webinar:write, report:read
  3. Click Activate Ticking permissions is not enough. If you skip Activate, nothing works, and the error will not tell you that.
  4. Put the Account ID, Client ID and Client Secret into your secrets
  5. Set webinar_id in Settings to your webinar number

Send from your own domain. Mail from your own address lands better and looks like you.

  1. Add your domain in Lovable’s email settings
  2. Add the DNS records it gives you, where you bought your domain
  3. Use a subdomain, not your bare domain For example mail.yourdomain.com. If a batch is ever marked as spam, the damage stays there and your normal email keeps working.

What comes after this

Next stage

WhatsApp reminders

For most audiences this outperforms email, and the funnel is already built for it: the database tables, the sending code and the schedule are all in place and switched off. What it needs is a WhatsApp Business provider and message templates approved by Meta in advance, which takes days rather than minutes. That is why it is its own stage rather than a step here. A separate guide will cover it.

Next stage

Your own dashboard

Every registration and sale already fires an event, and the funnel can post those to any address you give it. Nothing is connected yet, deliberately. When you have somewhere for them to go, it is one setting.
