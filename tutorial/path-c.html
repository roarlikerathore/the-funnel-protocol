Make the emails speak to each person

Everyone picked a situation and a struggle when they registered. This is what lets each email answer the one they chose. Six situations by five struggles means 30 versions of every email, from one piece of writing.

Skip this and the emails still send — everyone just gets the same generic one.

Paste into Lovable
Open supabase/seed-blocks.sql and fill in the block bodies. Write them in my
voice, using the exact profile and challenge wording already in
src/funnel.config.ts. Do not change any other file.

For EACH of my 6 profiles, write PROFILE_MIRROR: two or three short lines that
describe that person's actual day back to them. No advice, no pitch. It should
read like I have watched them live.

For EACH of my 6 profiles, write CTA_LINE: one line telling that specific person

For EACH of my 5 challenges, write PAIN_TWIST: two or three lines naming the

For EACH of my 5 challenges, write PROOF: two or three lines of reasoning for

6
Resend, then your domain10 min, then wait

Turn on email

Start this early. It is the single most common reason a funnel goes live and no email ever arrives, and part of it is out of your hands.

a. Verify your sending domain

  1. Open resend.com/domains → Add Domain
  2. Enter mail.yourdomain.com — the subdomain, not the bare domain
  3. Resend shows three records. Add them where you bought your domain
  4. Click Verify. Usually minutes, sometimes an hour
WHY

Send from a subdomain, never your main domain. If a batch ever gets marked as spam, the damage stays on mail.yourdomain.com and your normal business email keeps its clean reputation.

TRAP

Most domain hosts add your domain to whatever you type. If Resend shows send.mail.yourdomain.com, type only send.mail in the Name field. Typing the whole thing creates a record ending in your domain twice, and it silently never verifies.

b. Get your key

  1. resend.com/api-keys → Create API Key
  2. Copy it now. It is shown once.

c. Put it in Lovable

  1. Lovable → backend → Secrets
  2. RESEND_API_KEY = the key you just copied
  3. SENDER_EMAIL = hello@mail.yourdomain.com
SAFE

Secrets live in that panel and nowhere else. They never go in a file, and nothing there is readable from a visitor's browser.

When something goes wrong

In rough order of how often each actually happens.

No emails arrive

Three causes, in this order. One: your sending domain is not verified at resend.com/domains — it must say Verified, not Pending. Two: the scheduled jobs were never created; run SELECT jobname FROM cron.job; and you should see four. Three: it sent and went to spam. Check there before assuming failure.

Lovable used far more credits than expected

Something asked it to build rather than fill in. Every prompt here ends with "do not add features, do not redesign, do not touch other files" — that sentence is what keeps the cost down. If you dropped it, or asked for a design change in your own words, Lovable will happily rebuild things that already worked.

The page still says PLACEHOLDER

Those are fields you left blank or marked SKIP. Paste the step 3 prompt again with only the missing lines filled in, and add: "Update only these fields, leave everything else exactly as it is."

My domain will not verify

Almost always the doubled-name trap. Look at the record you created — if it ends in your domain twice, delete it and re-add with just the prefix. DNS can also take up to an hour, so if the record looks right, wait before changing anything.

Registration does not save

SETUP.sql did not finish. Re-run step 5 and read the output for an error rather than assuming it worked. It is safe to run more than once.

I want to change something later

Almost everything lives in one file. Tell Lovable: "In src/funnel.config.ts, change [field] to [value]. Change nothing else." Dates, prices, colours, copy — all one line, and every page and email updates with it.
