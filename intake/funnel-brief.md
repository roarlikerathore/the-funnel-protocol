# The Funnel Brief

Everything Claude needs to build your funnel. You can fill this in first, or let
Claude ask you these as questions — same result.

**How to fill it in:** write in plain language. Do not try to sound like a marketer.
The way you would explain it to a friend is usually the best version.

**If you do not know an answer:** write `SKIP`. Claude will use a placeholder and
list it in STILL-NEEDED.md so you can come back to it. Never invent a number or a
testimonial to fill a gap — a fake result is the fastest way to lose a room.
